# Hi, I'm Naireth
## About me
Final-year Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Antioquia, interested in multi-satellite space missions through the study and application of orbital mechanics and control, seeking to understand how the design and coordinated operation of multiple satellites can address challenges in space-based monitoring, communication, observation, and autonomous navigation across different space environments.

## Research Interest 
- Astrodynamics
- Orbital mechanics
- Spacecraft and dynamics control
- Spacecraft and state estimation
- Systems Engineering
- Autonomous space systems
- Multi-satellite mission analysis
- Communications
